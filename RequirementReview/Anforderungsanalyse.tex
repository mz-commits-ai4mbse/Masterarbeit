\subsection*{Detaillierte Systemanforderungen}

\begin{table}[ht]
\small
\centering
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{} l X X l @{}}
\toprule
\textbf{System-ID} & \textbf{Bezeichnung} & \textbf{System Requirement} & \textbf{Refines} \\
\midrule

SYS\_RQ\_1 & Multi-Format Ingestion &
The system shall implement specialized parsers for Excel, PDF, and CAD metadata fragments. &
SR\_1 \\

SYS\_RQ\_2 & JSON Normalization &
All ingested data fragments shall be normalized into a standardized JSON format to ensure interoperability. &
SR\_7 \\

SYS\_RQ\_3 & Data Slicing &
The system shall fragment standardized data into small, context-preserving packages for reliable LLM processing. &
SR\_1 \\

SYS\_RQ\_4 & Source Reliability Tagging &
Every data fragment shall be tagged with its source origin and a reliability score. &
SR\_6 \\

SYS\_RQ\_5 & Orchestrated Task Allocation &
The Orchestrator shall dynamically invoke specialized agents based on the data type and quality. &
SR\_1 \\

SYS\_RQ\_6 & Multi-Agent Persona Ensemble &
Each synthesis task shall be processed by at least three agents with varying temperatures and skills. &
SR\_1 \\

SYS\_RQ\_7 & Variance Quantification &
The system shall compute the statistical variance between results of the agent ensemble to assess reproducibility. &
SR\_5 \\

SYS\_RQ\_8 & Pattern Matching &
Specialized agents shall use an architectural pattern library to anticipate missing system structures. &
SR\_3 \\

SYS\_RQ\_9 & Semantic Conflict Resolution &
Contradictory information shall be resolved using a rule-based prioritization matrix. &
SR\_6, SR\_5 \\

SYS\_RQ\_10 & Modular Code Synthesis &
The system shall generate SysML v2 code using namespaces and usages to ensure plug-and-play modularity. &
SR\_2 \\

SYS\_RQ\_11 & Grammar Validation &
All generated SysML v2 fragments shall be validated against the formal KerML abstract syntax. &
SR\_1, SR\_5 \\

SYS\_RQ\_12 & Graphical User Interface &
The system shall provide a UI for managing data ingestion and reviewing synthesis results. &
SR\_4 \\

SYS\_RQ\_13 & Variance Reporting &
The UI shall highlight differences and similarities between agent results for expert evaluation. &
SR\_4 \\

SYS\_RQ\_14 & Expert Decision Gate &
The Orchestrator shall require manual confirmation before committing a low-confidence synthesis result. &
SR\_4 \\

SYS\_RQ\_15 & Traceability Logging &
The system shall maintain a log linking every generated SysML v2 element back to its source data fragment. &
SR\_5 \\

\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}
